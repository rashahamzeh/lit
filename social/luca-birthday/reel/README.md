# Birthday reel: source

`../5-birthday-reel.mp4` is 1080×1920, about 30 s, with an original piano score (synthesised in `music.py`, so it's royalty-free).

Built with `render.js` (renders text and scene layers from `layers.html`), then `music.py`, then `build.py` (ffmpeg).
The source photos and clips come from the LIT Google Drive. They are not committed here: Luca Cutout.png, the "Did You Know" reel (BTS and pointing frames),
the Best Shows of 2025 screenshot, and the luca-allam.com launch video (snippets at 2–5 s, 7–10 s and 20.4–25.4 s).
